The Great Mr. Dinkles

"Who's a guinea-guinea piggy? Who's a big, fat, chubby guinea pig? You are!

-Kulia, my sister (to her guinea pig, Mr. Dinkles).

"The Great Mr. Dinkles" is my sister's guinea pig. At about 3 years old, Mr. Dinkles has lived a lavish life. The obese brown rodent enjoys a plethora of pastimes within its mansion of a cage. Pastimes include:

  1. Sleeping
  2. Eating food
  3. Screaming at 2 am
  4. Judging people

In short, the little rodent loves just about anything and everything-- as long as that excludes physical excercise. Mr. Dinkles detests physical exercise. Instead, the creature loves to eat. Mr. Dinkles enjoys lettuce and carrots-- which I freely give it. After all, a fat guinea pig is much tastier than a scrawny guinea pig.
