The Ukulele-Making Process

Music is an important part of any culture. It is the heartbeat of dance, ceremonies, and gatherings of every kind-- and cultures' musical instruments often facilitate cultural exchange.

The ukulele is one of the world's most recognizable instruments-- and for good reason. Easy to pick up, and pleasant to the ear, the Hawaiian instrument is now used by musicians all around the world!
