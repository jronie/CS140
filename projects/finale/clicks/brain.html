The Effects of the Ukulele on the Brain Development of Children

A study published in the journal "PLoS One" investigated the effects of weekly 40-minute music classes on public school children throughout an entire school year-- during which, the students were taught to play the ukulele. The study, performed on 84 third and fourth-graders, resulted in the following:
