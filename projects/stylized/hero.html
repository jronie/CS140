Mikaele Oloa

Hero Interest Page

Mikaele Oloa is a multi-title world champion fireknife dancer. Having revolutionized fireknife dancing with his unique dancing style, he has popularized complicated tosses and misdirection used by many world compettitors of the dance. Compteting in contests, I've had the ocassion to meet and speak with Mikaele. He's an amazing dancer and man.
