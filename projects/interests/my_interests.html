Fireknife Dancing

Hero Interest Page




Fireknife dancing is a dance which has been in my family for generations. I was taught to dance by my dad, who was taught by my grandfather. It's a passion which I enjoy participating in and have used to travel all over the world

In addition to my father, grandfather, and I dancing fireknife, my cousins and uncles also dance. I have fond memories of dancing together at large cultural festivals-- sharing a message of family unity. In my family, the dance is seen as a way to grow closer to each other and also God.
