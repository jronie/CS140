Do you want to work on projects, but can't do it alone? Are you looking for other creative minds? Do you wish that there was a way to get in touch with other local creators? Is what you want to learn not found on other major networking websites?

HanaWiki is a non-profit networking site which allows you to get into contact with other creative minds around you. Looking for a tutor of a cultural craft you can't find online? At HanaWiki, we facilitate contacts with over 100 cultural practitioners.
